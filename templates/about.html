About SignSpeak AI

Breaking communication barriers by turning sign language gestures into readable text and natural voice.

Turn Sign Language Into Voice

SignSpeak AI is an assistive communication application designed to make everyday conversations seamless between individuals who use sign language and those who do not.

Using real-time computer vision and deep learning, the app identifies American Sign Language (ASL) gestures from your webcam, assembles them into words, and speaks them out loud. For moments when camera access isn't available, our interactive Sign Board provides visual cards to tap and speak effortlessly.

100% Private

Video feeds are processed in real-time in your browser session. Your video is never recorded or stored.

Highly Accessible

Built with high contrast, large buttons, clean vector sign cards, and smooth screen-reader support.

Fast & Instant

Instant speech synthesis and low-latency gesture detection designed for practical conversation.

Language Scope Notice

SignSpeak AI focuses on American Sign Language (ASL) alphabet finger-spelling and essential everyday communication signs. It does not replace full natural sentence sign language, but provides an accessible bridge for everyday words.
